# Using Your Agent

A page to come back to. Your agent can also explain any of this — just ask it.

## Projects

Anything you're working on — planning a trip, a renovation, your taxes, a side business — can be a project. To start one, tell your agent: "set up a project for the kitchen renovation." It makes a folder for it in your projects folder.

Then open it: in a new Claude Code window, go to that folder and type `claude` (your agent will tell you exactly what to type). Your agent is there, already knowing the project. It keeps notes as you go — where things stand, what's left, what it has learned — so next time you can just say "where were we?"

A folder that isn't one of your agent's projects is plain Claude Code, as it always was.

## Talking to It

- **On your computer:** in Claude Code, in your agent's folder or any project.
- **On your phone:** on Signal. Your direct chat with it is for anything. You can also have a group chat per project — ask your agent to set one up — and in that chat it's working on that project.
- **Voice notes and photos** work on Signal: send them like you would to anyone.

Signal is also how your agent gets in touch with you first: a reminder you asked for, a routine's result, or something that needs your attention. It's the same agent everywhere, with the same memory. It answers wherever you asked: on Signal, or in Claude Code.

On a longer task it tells you what it's doing as it goes, and each reply on Signal ends with how long it took. Each night, after it has saved what the day taught it, every Signal chat starts a fresh conversation, so the first message of the morning starts clean and quick.

## Email

Your agent has its own email address (it's in its memory; ask it). Forward it things — a receipt, a booking, a document — and ask it to take a look. It can email you too, whenever you ask, or on a schedule.

It only reads its inbox when you ask it to (or when you've asked it to check on a schedule). It reads everyone's email but only takes instructions from yours.

## Scheduling

Just ask:

- "Remind me at 10pm to call Mum."
- "Every Monday at 8am, tell me what my week looks like."
- "Check your email every morning and tell me on Signal if anything needs me."
- "Every Friday, email me a summary of what we got done."

It runs while your Mac is awake. Anything it missed while the Mac slept, it does when the Mac wakes.

## Keep Your Mac Plugged In, Lid Open

Your agent lives on your Mac, so it only works while the Mac is awake. Plugged in with the lid open, it stays awake on its own — the screen can still turn off. On battery or with the lid shut, the Mac sleeps: messages wait, and your agent catches up when it wakes. If it's been unplugged for half an hour, your agent tells you on Signal, while it still can.

## Memory

You don't do anything. Before every message, your agent looks back through everything you've told it and everything it has written, and reminds itself of what's relevant. Every night it "dreams": it reads the day and keeps tidy notes, by subject, of what it has learned. It all stays on your Mac.

It gets better the more you use it. In the first few weeks it knows little; after a while it remembers the things you'd otherwise have to repeat.

## Errands on the Web

If you set up the browser, your agent can look things up and do errands on websites — open times, availability, prices — in its own Chrome instance, with its own data folder, separate from any browser you use. It asks before buying, booking, submitting or sending anything. For a site that needs an account, ask it to open its Chrome for you; sign in once, and it stays signed in there, on this Mac only. During a task a Chrome icon may sit in the Dock looking frozen: leave it, it's working.

## Syncing to GitHub

If you chose one at setup, your agent's folder and your projects are synced to private repositories on GitHub every night, so a lost or broken Mac doesn't take what your agent has learned with it. Only you can see them. If you didn't, and want one now, ask your agent to sync to GitHub.

## Two Things Worth Knowing

- **"Check for updates"** — when Tangram improves, your agent tells you what's new and asks whether you want each change. You can also ask it any time.
- **When something breaks** — say Signal stops answering — your agent tells you on Signal or the next time you talk to it, and says what to do.
